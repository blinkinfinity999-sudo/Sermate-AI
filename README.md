# 🤖 SerMate AI

> **Your AI-Powered Floating Screen Companion**  
> SerMate AI is an interactive, floating companion powered by **Gemini Vision**. It sits on top of your screen to analyze content, answer questions, and assist you seamlessly while you work, browse, or code.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌟 **System-Wide Floating Overlay:** Stays pinned in the bottom-right corner over all your apps, games, and windows.
- 🖼️ **Gemini Vision Integration:** Snap your screen or ask questions directly to analyze visuals in real-time.
- 🪟 **Pop Out Widget (Document PiP):** Use the web PWA and pop out the floating widget directly over your desktop without downloading an `.exe`.
- ⚡ **Seamless & Frameless:** Modern, borderless transparent interface.
- ⌨️ **Global Hotkeys:** Quick keyboard shortcuts to capture and interact on the fly.

---

## 🚀 How to Use SerMate AI

### Option A: Web PWA (No Download Required)
1. Visit [SerMate AI Web](https://blinkinfinity999-sudo.github.io/Sermate-AI/).
2. Click **Install App** in your browser's address bar to install as a PWA.
3. Open SerMate AI and click **"Pop Out Widget"**.
4. 💥 **BOOM!** The widget detaches into a floating window in the bottom-right corner of your monitor!

### Option B: Native Desktop Companion (`.exe`)
1. Download the latest `SerMate-AI-Setup.exe` from the **[Releases](../../releases)** tab.
2. Double-click the downloaded file (instant silent setup).
3. The frameless widget will automatically launch in your screen's bottom-right corner.
